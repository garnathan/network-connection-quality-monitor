## Verdict

**Carrier rate-policing, no local queueing.** Neither hop shows latency rise. The carrier is dropping excess traffic rather than buffering it. Router is not the cause.

### Decision key

- **Router bottleneck**: Eero Δ large, modem Δ small — the queue is inside the Eero.
- **Modem/carrier bottleneck**: Both Δ large — queue is at/above the modem, the Eero spike just reflects upstream congestion.
- **Carrier rate-policing**: Both Δ small but throughput still collapses — carrier drops excess rather than queueing.

Thresholds: Δavg > 50 ms OR Δstddev > 20 ms counts as a spike.
